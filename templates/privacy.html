Privacy

What BanaoBot collects, and why.

What we collect

Account details you provide when you sign up, such as your name and email address.

Bot configuration you provide: the prompts, menus, FAQs, settings, and API key you enter to set up your bot. Your API key is stored encrypted.

Bot usage data needed to run your bots: the messages your bots handle, and the bookings and owner alerts they create.

Why we collect it

This information is used to create your account, run your bots, and show you their activity in your dashboard.
