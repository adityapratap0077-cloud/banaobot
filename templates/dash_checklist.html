Go-live checklist

Everything needed to connect {{ biz.name }} to a real WhatsApp Business number. Do it once, in order.

  1. Meta Business Manager account + verification

    Create an account at business.facebook.com, add your business details, and complete business verification (Meta asks for documents like a GST certificate, utility bill or business licence). Verification takes a few days — start this first, everything else waits on it.

  2. Get a dedicated phone number

    You need a phone number that has never been used on WhatsApp (regular or Business app). A fresh SIM works. During setup Meta will call or SMS this number with a verification code, so keep it reachable. You cannot reuse your personal WhatsApp number.

  3. Create a Meta developer app + connect the number

    At developers.facebook.com/apps: create an app (Business type) → add the WhatsApp product → follow “API Setup” to register your dedicated number. Copy the phone number ID and your WhatsApp config ID — paste the config ID into META_CONFIG_ID (and the app ID into META_APP_ID) at the top of the Connect page script.

  4. Configure the webhook

    In your Meta app → WhatsApp → Configuration, set the webhook so Meta can deliver customer messages to this server:

    • Callback URL: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/webhook (replace YOUR-DOMAIN with your real domain, HTTPS required)
    • Verify token: the value of the VERIFY_TOKEN environment variable on this server
    • Subscribe to the messages field

    Then click Verify and save — Meta will call the URL once to check the token matches.

  5. Create a system-user access token

    In Business Manager → Business settings → System users: create a system user, give it the WhatsApp Business account asset, and generate a token with the whatsapp_business_messaging permission (never expiring, stored encrypted). You already have the phone number ID from step 3. Either complete the Connect with Facebook button (Embedded Signup does this automatically) or paste both values via Option B on the Connect page.

  6. App review for the two WhatsApp permissions

    For production (messaging real customers beyond test numbers), Meta requires app review for:

    • whatsapp_business_management — managing the business account & numbers
    • whatsapp_business_messaging — sending/receiving customer messages

    Prepare a short screencast showing a customer chatting with the bot and the owner receiving a handoff alert — reviewers approve fastest when they can see the real user flow.

Done with the checklist? Head back and connect.
