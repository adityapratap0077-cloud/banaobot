? How to get your brain key

The key is free from Google. Five minutes, once, and every bot on your account wakes up.

  1. Go to Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.

  2. Click Get API key (top-left), then Create API key.

  3. Copy the key — it starts with AIza. Keep it private, like a password.

  4. Paste it in the box above and hit Save key.

  5. Done — open any bot and start chatting. Remove or replace the key here any time.

Google's free tier covers casual use. If you ever hit a quota limit, the bot will tell you honestly instead of making things up.
